Re-Styled with FORM

Social media content pitch for Rutgers FORM, 2026

I noticed that videos about restyling thrifted clothes keep spreading on social media. I pitched a member-powered content series to FORM, a Rutgers fashion business club, and designed a mock post to show how it would look in their feed.

What I proposed

  • Members submit short clips restyling a thrifted or existing piece into a new outfit, as a weekly or daily feature.
  • Styling challenges, like "restyle this for under $15," with members voting through Instagram polls.
  • A future partnership with a local thrift store for shoutouts or prizes.

The idea is low-cost, ties into the sustainable retail conversation, and gives the club steady content that comes from members instead of only the e-board.

Forage Merchandising Job Simulation

Completed September 2026, with a written reflection for class

In this simulation I stepped into the role of a merchandising analyst preparing a seasonal product launch. I completed four tasks that connect data, design, and strategy.

What I did

  • Built an on-order log with Excel pivot tables. Breaking orders down by channel showed that European customers buy more online, while Canadian shoppers prefer stores.
  • Designed a line sheet for Canadian store teams that presents styles, colors, pricing, and quantities clearly.
  • Calculated full-price sell-through to spot which products were beating targets and which needed action, like a new placement or a promotion.
  • Compared the strategies of Alo Yoga and Athleta in a competitor analysis.
